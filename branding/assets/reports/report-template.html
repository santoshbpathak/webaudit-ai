Methodology

Pages are discovered from robots.txt, sitemaps and internal links. HTTP checks run on every fetched page; browser checks (performance, accessibility, tags, forms, UX, consent) run on a stratified sample. Issues are grouped by rule and inferred page template and ranked by a transparent priority index: severity × reach × confidence × importance.

Limitations

  • Automated checks cannot establish legal compliance (privacy, consent, accessibility) or guarantee ranking, traffic or conversion outcomes.
  • Performance numbers are Lighthouse lab measurements, not real-user field data.
  • Tags gated behind consent, logins, ad blockers or later user actions may not be observed.
  • 1 URL(s) were excluded by robots.txt and not audited.

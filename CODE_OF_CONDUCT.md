# Code of conduct

WebAudit AI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Marketers, developers and specialists bring different expertise, and all of it is welcome.
- Welcome newcomers and explain jargon.
- Critique ideas, rules and code, not people.
- Never share someone else's private data, or private or client website information.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and doxxing are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@santoshbpathak](https://github.com/santoshbpathak), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or restrict participation, in line with the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
